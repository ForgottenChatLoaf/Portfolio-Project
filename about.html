About Me

Who I Am.

I'm an IT student who started programming through college coursework and gradually discovered how much I enjoy it. What began as school activities turned into an interest in experimenting with code, solving problems, and building things of my own.

I enjoy learning by doing, whether that's figuring out a programming challenge, experimenting with game ideas, or working on a web project. I've worked with C, explored game development in Roblox Studio, and built websites using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I'm also learning PHP and MySQL as I work on projects that involve backend development.

I'm still learning and figuring things out along the way, but that's part of what makes programming fun for me. I enjoy seeing an idea come to life, learning from mistakes, and finding ways to make each project a little better.
